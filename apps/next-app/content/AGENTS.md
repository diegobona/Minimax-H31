# MiniMax site content instructions

Before creating or editing files in this directory, read and follow [SITE-RULES.md](./SITE-RULES.md).

For public pages, use one `<slug>.ts` file per page and export structured English content. Apply the site's source-verification, H3.1 uncertainty, keyword ownership, word-count, table, FAQ, date, and internal-link requirements. Preserve the original rule numbering and do not invent missing product facts or keyword assignments.

SITE-RULES.md is an internal record of user requirements, not an official model announcement or publishable evidence. Its site-specific rules take precedence over conflicting generic TinyShip defaults.
